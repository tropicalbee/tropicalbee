# Art direction

Created using the built-in image-generation tool in edit mode. The exact user-selected garage image was the edit target. The accepted Resident section was supplied only as the ghost and README style reference.

## Final edit prompt

Edit the selected wide 8:3 haunted garage. Preserve the oak rafters, cauldron over fire in the left third, brewing equipment at left, blank oak sign at upper right center, jack-o-lantern at lower right and dark doorway at far right.

Use authentic chunky low-resolution Minecraft pixel blocks: obvious large stone modules, coarse oak pixels, flat cuboid iron cauldron with thick square rim and square legs, crisp stepped contours, restricted palette, and large dark stone floor tiles. Remove smooth cinematic lighting, fine photographic texture, soft gradients, blur and polished fantasy rendering.

Add a clearly visible full-body pale ivory ghost immediately left of the cauldron. Match the Resident's broad flat stepped dome, squared sides, small dark vertical rectangular eyes, tiny olive mouth pixel, cream highlight, gray-green side shadow and three blocky sheet tails. No laptop in this brewing scene.

Make the garage eerie with deep charcoal and midnight navy shadows, a near-black doorway with tiny red eyes, an angular carved pumpkin, sparse dirty moss, one proper torn pixel cobweb, a small amber fire and a thin muted poison-green potion surface. Avoid purple, neon, fog, books and skulls. Leave the sign completely blank for editable native lettering. No bubbles baked into the background.

## Editable additions

- Large pixel heading: `PROJECTS`. Smaller status: `still brewing in my garage..`.
- Hollow pixel potion bubbles, rising and popping.
- A small ripple clipped to the liquid surface.
- Bright-core masks for fire and pumpkin flicker; all room geometry remains still.

## Palette

Charcoal and midnight navy masonry, dark oak, subdued ivory lettering and ghost, muted amber/orange light, sparse moss and potion green. No purple or broad bloom.
